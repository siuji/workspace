I co-founded Konkrë in 2021 with 2 partners, to position concrete as a high-end design material. It became Brazil's pioneer in colored concrete sinks, present in landmark architecture projects, exporting, and recognized at national and international design awards. Payback came in less than 6 months from the start of operations.

Today my role is management, not day-to-day operations. Product creation and development, business management (finance, HR, accounting) and the strategic vision of the company are with me.

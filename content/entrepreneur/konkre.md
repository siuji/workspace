Konkrë is a Brazilian company that designs and makes architectural concrete pieces. I co-founded it in 2021 with 2 partners, and it became the country's pioneer in colored concrete sinks.

At our factory in Vila Romana, São Paulo, we design and make by hand sinks, washbasins, countertops, vases and planters. The color is mixed into the concrete itself, never painted.

Our goal is to bring concrete into spaces and close to people, combining technical rigor and handmade craft in signature pieces with their own presence and identity.

Today we serve architects, interior designers, stores, builders and end clients across Brazil, and we already export. Our work received the iF Design Award 2026 and silver at Prêmio Brasil Design.

My role today is management, not day-to-day operations. Product creation and development, business management (finance, HR, accounting) and the strategic vision of the company are with me.

<p>You'll find a bit of me on both profiles. But I'll be honest: content creation is not my strongest skill. I'm much better at making things than at posting about them.</p>
<p>Instagram is my big life album. Trips, races, family, the moments worth keeping. Today it's also the easiest way to stay close to a lot of people.</p>
<p>LinkedIn turned into my professional network, the place where {{years}} years of colleagues, clients and partners live.</p>
<p>Being active online still doesn't come naturally to me. That doesn't mean I'm not there. I'm always reading, learning and admiring the people who built a real voice on social media. It's one part of the craft I haven't mastered yet.</p>

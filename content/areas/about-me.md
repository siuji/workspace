<p>Married, dad of 2 boys and a pet dad. Home is busy, and I like it that way. I'm curious by nature. I like to understand how things work, try what's new, and I'm not afraid to start over. Travel is where that curiosity feels most alive. I've walked pilgrimage routes like the Camino de Santiago, the Inca Trail and the Caminho da Fé, crossed the Andes by bike from Argentina to Chile, and lost count of the trips through the Brazilian countryside. During the pandemic, while the world was in isolation, I needed air, so I walked from Ouro Preto to Diamantina along the Estrada Real. Sometimes photography is the excuse, sometimes it's a marathon somewhere new. Sometimes it's time alone, sometimes it's time with my family. New places and new cultures are what make me happiest. Speaking Portuguese, English and Spanish helps a lot along the way.</p>

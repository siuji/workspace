Quest Diagnostics is a diagnostic testing company. Through its app, people book tests and get their results right on their phone. A business built on making people's lives easier, which is exactly what AI does best.

This was a single film, made entirely with AI, and it followed the standards of a real advertising production. Casting, wardrobe, art direction, cinematography: every step was planned the way it would be on a live-action shoot.

That's what makes AI so remarkable. Brands that don't have the budget for a full production can now make films that truly meet their needs.

The effects and the result speak for themselves. A film that's hard to believe isn't real.

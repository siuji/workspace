A very special project. Pfizer wanted to talk openly with people going through medical treatment and give them real support, inside one of the most regulated markets there is.

We created more than 100 videos, each in a different visual style, telling stories that help people through their treatments. Every film respects the strict rules of pharma communication, without losing warmth or beauty.

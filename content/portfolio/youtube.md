As Group Creative Director at Monks, 100% dedicated to YouTube's global structure, my main role has been to lead a diverse and plural team, leveraging talents from all corners of the world.

The team runs holistic programs across performance media, email, in-product notifications and websites. By developing efficient end-to-end processes, optimizing strategy and operations, creative output, project management, and channel performance and analytics in all things YouTube.

Supporting the global team, I took special care of LATAM and of YouTube products such as Gaming, Kids and, most recently, the whole Artists operation.

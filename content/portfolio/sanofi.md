When was the last time you saw an ad that was truly tailored to a specific platform?

Sanofi is a global account that requires localizing its campaigns across many countries. Our team brought a more Brazilian approach to the creatives, making them more relevant to the local audience.

Beyond the standard rollout for Novalgina and Bisolvon, we adapted the campaigns for TikTok, using music and the platform's own voice message feature to create playful versions of the content, fully customized for this new context.

The work became a showcase for the client, and an example for other countries where Sanofi operates.

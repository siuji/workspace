A bold new smash burger brand designed to be youthful, inspiring and ready to grow.

As creative lead, I partnered closely with the client to uncover key insights and turn them into a powerful brand identity. In just 3 months, a multidisciplinary team delivered a strategic and visually striking brand that stands out in a crowded market and captures the energy of a new generation.

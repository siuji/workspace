At Macy's, we're using AI to change the way the brand works. We create proofs of concept, from the concept to the full aesthetic, much faster and more practically. That gives Macy's the material it needs to get internal approvals, before moving into the real productions.

<h3 class="sector">One Day Sale</h3>

<div class="video"><iframe src="https://player.vimeo.com/video/1249012244?autopause=0&amp;dnt=1" title="Macy's One Day Sale, AI mood film" frameborder="0" allow="autoplay; fullscreen; picture-in-picture; clipboard-write; encrypted-media; web-share" referrerpolicy="strict-origin-when-cross-origin" allowfullscreen loading="lazy"></iframe></div>

<p class="caption">The AI mood film, made at the agency</p>

For Macy's One Day Sale, we made a mood film in record time: just 24 hours. Every image you see, the edit, the soundtrack, the whole film, created with AI in a single day.

Its job was to approve the creative direction and the tone of the campaign, and to serve as a guide for the production. With it, Macy's went through all of its internal approvals, and only then moved into the real shoot.

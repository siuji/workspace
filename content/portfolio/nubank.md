One of the largest digital financial services platforms in the world ran a campaign about its life insurance. People avoid this conversation, even though the service matters. The goal was to change that narrative and make insurance a real, shareable symbol of care and love.

So we made a campaign about love in all its forms. Talking about life insurance is also talking about caring for the people we love: showing affection in the present while ensuring some comfort for the future.

Around 2 months of social media and audience interaction: memes, videos and educational content, with activations on YouTube, TikTok, Meta and DV360.

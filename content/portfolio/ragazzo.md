A big Brazilian fast-food chain. Leading a complete creative squad fully dedicated to the client, we invested in the craft of the productions to reverse the promotional image that was strongly tied to the brand.

With a new menu, all restaurant materials and a comprehensive on and off media campaign, we repositioned this traditional Italian fast-food brand.

It was the first time Monks handled a full delivery, from digital campaigns to TV commercials and in-store menus. I was one of the main leaders, with a very close relationship with the client.

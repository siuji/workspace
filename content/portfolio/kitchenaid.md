How do you create hard-sell communication for a highly sophisticated brand?

We answered with creativity and sensitivity, creating campaigns and pieces that could be an extension of what people would like to see. The best of branding combined with expressive performance communication.

In Brazil, KitchenAid is imported by Whirlpool. Most of the work was localization, and sometimes new local campaigns.

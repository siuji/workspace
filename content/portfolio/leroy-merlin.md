One of the biggest wins of my time as Group Creative Director at Monks. I worked actively on winning the account, and then on building a system that completely changed the way the client itself worked.

Leroy Merlin Brazil, one of Brazil's largest home improvement retailers, with over 50 stores, asked for more than a digital-first agency. We had to become its full Agency of Record. With new AI capabilities, we scaled ideas, streamlined production and accelerated delivery across media.

<div class="stats"><div><b>2×</b><span>faster creative development with AI tools</span></div><div><b>30%</b><span>less headcount for the same volume as the previous agency</span></div><div><b>3×</b><span>faster copy creation</span></div></div>

**A design system for everyone.** We built a design system able to handle the complexity of every material the client needed. It served the agency's internal team, and also Leroy Merlin's own team and its partners. It changed the way Leroy Merlin works, and became the base for teams in other countries.

It was the first project with a truly AI-driven way of thinking from the start, and that is what brought this client to Monks.

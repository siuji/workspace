Leading the digital transformation for Leroy Merlin Brazil, a prominent home improvement retailer with over 50 stores, presented a unique challenge: evolving our digital-first agency into a full-fledged Agency of Record (AOR). Leveraging new AI capabilities, we scaled ideas, streamlined production, and balanced digital agility with traditional craft.

The core objectives: streamline internal and external processes, build a readily accessible database of images and videos, and accelerate delivery across media. Save time and human capital by optimizing asset creation, boost organic social results through trend responsiveness, and improve communication between marketing and design teams.

I was involved from the client acquisition and helped set up the whole operation. It was the first project with a truly AI-driven way of thinking from the start, and that is what brought this client to Monks.

Compra Certa is Whirlpool's e-commerce platform for home items. It started as a private buying group. The strategic decision to serve the entire public required new guidelines, and we transformed the brand's communication and processes.

We created a repositioning that updated its identity and the way it relates to the audience. With a comprehensive asset bank, we brought new life to the website, social media and blog. The transformation generated significant sales results and improved brand perception.

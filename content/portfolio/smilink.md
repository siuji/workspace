Smilink, a company that makes orthodontic aligners, came to the agency asking only for a simple set of performance banners. A vague brief, limited knowledge of the local market, an uncertain budget, and well-established competitors.

I sensed early on that they could, and should, do much more. So I got closer to the client to understand what they really needed, and presented a more ambitious plan: a brand guide for consistency, a full digital campaign across the whole media funnel, and new formats, including radio spots and a TV commercial.

A tactical request turned into a fully integrated campaign, and a trusted, ongoing client relationship.
